# Delete your SideDish account

Need to permanently delete your SideDish account and associated data?

## In the app is fastest

If you can sign in, use **Profile → Delete account**. This permanently deletes
your account, profile, signatures, account-linked history, and associated
private uploads.

## Cannot access the app?

Send an email from the address on your SideDish account. Include the account
email and confirm that you understand the request is permanent. We use your
message only to verify ownership and process deletion.

[Email deletion request](mailto:jonathan@nullmening.org?subject=SideDish%20account%20deletion%20request&body=Please%20delete%20my%20SideDish%20account%20and%20associated%20personal%20data.%0A%0AAccount%20email%3A%20%0A%0AI%20understand%20this%20request%20is%20permanent.)

## Guest accounts without an email

Guest accounts have no account email. If you still have the original session,
use **Profile → Delete account** in the app. If you have lost access,
[email a guest-account deletion request](mailto:jonathan@nullmening.org?subject=SideDish%20guest%20account%20deletion)
from an address where you can receive replies. Say that you used a guest account
and include any account reference you already have, your profile name, and a
table code with approximate dates to help us locate it. We still need to verify
ownership; a profile name alone is not proof, and we may be unable to locate or
verify a guest account after its session is lost.

## What deletion means

- Deletion cannot be undone.
- Deleting your account does not cancel an Apple or Google subscription. Cancel
  SideDish Pro in your store subscription settings to stop renewal before
  deleting your account.
- Contact us for deletion requests involving subscription-provider records or
  previously forwarded support messages. Some payment records may need to be
  retained for legal obligations.
- We erase account-linked data unless a short, limited retention period is
  necessary for fraud prevention, payment disputes, or legal duties.
- For more information, read the [Privacy Policy](../privacy/).
