Account and data deletion

Delete your account

Need to permanently delete your SideDish account and associated data?

In the app is fastest

If you can sign in, use Profile → Delete account. This permanently deletes your account, profile, signatures, account-linked history, and associated private uploads.

Cannot access the app?

Send an email from the address on your SideDish account. Include the account email and confirm that you understand the request is permanent. We use your message only to verify ownership and process deletion.

Email deletion request

What deletion means

  • Deletion cannot be undone.
  • We erase account-linked data unless a short, limited retention period is necessary for fraud prevention, payment disputes, or legal duties.
  • For more information, read the Privacy Policy.
