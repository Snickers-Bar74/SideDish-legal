# Privacy Policy — SideDish

**Last updated:** 2026-10-23
**Contact:** jonathan@nullmening.org

This policy explains how SideDish handles data to run accounts, live table
games, support, SideDish Pro, optional rewarded ads, and first-party product
analytics.

---

## Controller and contact

Jonathan Berentsen, Åsgårdstrandsveien 372, 3179 Åsgårdstrand, Norway, is
responsible for processing personal data in SideDish. Contact
[jonathan@nullmening.org](mailto:jonathan@nullmening.org) for privacy questions
or requests.

## Data we process

### Account and sign-in

Your account identifier and email address, plus authentication information
handled by Supabase. If you choose Google Sign-In, Google also processes the
sign-in.

### Profile and game data

Your display name, selected profile choices, favourites, game statistics, table
details, player names and colours, scores, timestamps, game selections, answers,
votes, drawings, and other content submitted while playing.

### Support reports

The category and message you send, optional reply email or screenshot, app
version, and platform. Safety reports can include a table reference needed to
investigate conduct or content.

### Purchases, rewards, and diagnostics

Apple or Google processes payments. We receive a pseudonymous app user ID,
product, entitlement status, expiry date, and transaction identifier to unlock
Pro. Optional rewarded ads use AdMob verification data. Sentry receives crash
and performance diagnostics such as device, operating system, app version, error
context, and traces.

### First-party product analytics

We store limited event names for onboarding completion, hosting, joining,
starting or finishing a game, table completion, paywall views, purchases,
restores, and rewarded-ad outcomes in our own Supabase project. These events are
linked to your account identifier and are pseudonymous, not anonymous. They
include time, app version, and platform, but not names, room codes, messages,
game answers, precise location, advertising identifiers, or device identifiers.
These analytics are off until you enable them in your profile. You can turn
optional product analytics off anytime in your profile. The same switch controls
an anonymous copy of these events, plus app opens, leaving a table, queuing or
abandoning a game and adding or removing a player, sent to PostHog, hosted in
the EU, without your account identifier, a stored device identifier or location
lookup.

## Photos, camera, and local storage

Camera access is used only when you choose to scan a QR code or take a profile
photo; QR frames are processed on the device and are not saved. Photo-library
access is used only when you choose a profile photo or attach a screenshot to
feedback — profile photos are stored with your profile so they can be shown to
people at the same table, and feedback screenshots are uploaded with the report
you send. The app also keeps a local cache of profile, preferences, and table
data on your device so it can reopen and recover from a connection loss; you can
clear local history in Profile.

## Why and how long

- We use data to secure accounts, run live games, support users, prevent misuse,
  process Pro and rewards, improve reliability, and meet legal obligations.
- Account and profile data remain until account deletion.
- Inactive live tables automatically close one hour after the last activity.
  Their records follow the closed-table retention period below.
- Feedback and screenshots are deleted after 14 days, or sooner on account
  deletion. Safety reports are kept up to 30 days.
- Closed table records are normally deleted after 30 days. They may be retained
  for up to two years while a participant has an active Pro subscription.
- First-party analytics events are deleted after 90 days.

## Who receives data

People at the same table can see information needed to play. SideDish has no
public profile directory or public feed. We do not sell personal data. Service
providers include Supabase (authentication, database, storage and realtime),
Google (optional sign-in and AdMob), Sentry (diagnostics), PostHog (anonymous
product analytics, EU), RevenueCat (subscription status), Apple and Google
(payments), and Discord for private, non-safety feedback delivery. The Discord
relay excludes your reply email.

### Ad choices and ratings

Where required, Google presents consent choices before a rewarded ad can load.
Available choices can be changed in the app through Google’s privacy options.
Personalized ads require the relevant Google consent and, on iOS, tracking
permission; otherwise ad requests are non-personalized. Store review prompts are
shown through Apple or Google only after a completed session; SideDish does not
receive the rating or review you submit.

## Your rights and choices

You can update profile data, export account data, or delete your account in the
app. If you cannot access the app, use the deletion request page below. You can
also request access, correction, deletion, objection, or other privacy help at
[jonathan@nullmening.org](mailto:jonathan@nullmening.org). You may complain to
your local data-protection authority; in Norway this is Datatilsynet.

[Request account deletion](../delete-account/)

## Children

SideDish is a 16+ service and is not directed to children under 16. We do not
ask for or store a date of birth. By creating an account or continuing as a
guest, a user confirms that they are at least 16; we retain only that
confirmation timestamp. This is a self-attestation, not age verification. If you
believe someone under 16 has created an account, contact us and we will
investigate and remove the account where appropriate.

## Changes

We update the date above when this policy changes. If a change materially
affects how we use data, we provide notice before it takes effect where
required.
