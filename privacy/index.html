Privacy Policy

This policy explains how SideDish handles data to run accounts, live table games, support, SideDish Pro, and optional rewarded ads.

Controller and contact

Jonathan Berentsen, Åsgårdstrandsveien 372, 3179 Åsgårdstrand, Norway, is responsible for processing personal data in SideDish. Contact jonathan@nullmening.org for privacy questions or requests.

Data we process

Account and sign-in

Your account identifier and email address, plus authentication information handled by Supabase. If you choose Google Sign-In, Google also processes the sign-in.

Profile and game data

Your display name, selected profile choices, favourites, game statistics, table details, player names and colours, scores, timestamps, game selections, answers, votes, drawings, and other content submitted while playing.

Support reports

The category and message you send, optional reply email or screenshot, app version, and platform. Safety reports can include a table reference needed to investigate conduct or content.

Purchases, rewards, and diagnostics

Apple or Google processes payments. We receive a pseudonymous app user ID, product, entitlement status, expiry date, and transaction identifier to unlock Pro. Optional rewarded ads use AdMob verification data. Sentry receives crash and performance diagnostics such as device, operating system, app version, error context, and traces.

Why and how long

  • We use data to secure accounts, run live games, support users, prevent misuse, process Pro and rewards, and meet legal obligations.
  • Account and profile data remain until account deletion.
  • Live tables and game data are deleted one hour after the last activity.
  • Feedback and screenshots are deleted after 14 days, or sooner on account deletion. Safety reports are kept up to 30 days.
  • Closed table records are deleted after 30 days.

Who receives data

People at the same table can see information needed to play. SideDish has no public profile directory or public feed. We do not sell personal data. Service providers include Supabase (authentication, database, storage and realtime), Google (optional sign-in and AdMob), Sentry (diagnostics), RevenueCat (subscription status), Apple and Google (payments), and Discord for private, non-safety feedback delivery. The Discord relay excludes your reply email.

Your rights and choices

You can update profile data, export account data, or delete your account in the app. If you cannot access the app, use the deletion request page below. You can also request access, correction, deletion, objection, or other privacy help at jonathan@nullmening.org. You may complain to your local data-protection authority; in Norway this is Datatilsynet.

Request account deletion

Children

SideDish is a 16+ service and is not directed to children. We do not ask for or store a date of birth; users confirm they are at least 16 before using the app.

Changes

We update the date above when this policy changes. If a change materially affects how we use data, we provide notice before it takes effect where required.
